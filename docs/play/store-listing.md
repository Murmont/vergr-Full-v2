VERGR is where gamers and creators go live, share clips and hang out with the people who get it.

Go live right from your phone, no streaming software needed, or hook up OBS on your PC. Your viewers can chat, tip and clip the best moments as they happen, whether you're gaming, making something or just talking.

VERGR is free. Lite and Pro take the ads away and add things like better stream quality and bigger uploads.

VERGR is for ages 18 and up.
