Reading the table:
- Cards in the US / UK / EU run 2.6–4.75% **plus** a fixed fee (US $0.25–0.28, UK £0.20, EU €0.23). On a
  $0.99 pack the fixed fee alone is about a quarter of the price.
- Carrier billing and some local wallets run 20–38.5% (Myanmar 38.5, Mongolia 34.7, Thailand 34,
  Indonesia 32, Malaysia / Philippines / Bangladesh 29, Laos 24, Vietnam 22, Taiwan 20.8).
- "X% VAT" is deducted from our price (Coda's sales-tax page), so local prices must be grossed up
  for it, as the Play prices are.
