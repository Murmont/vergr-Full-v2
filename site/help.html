rocket_launch Getting Started

Sign up using your Google account — just tap "Sign in with Google" on the login screen. Your profile is created instantly. From there, you can add a username, bio, profile picture, and select your favourite games.
VERGR is available right now as a web app — just open it in your browser. A native Windows desktop app (via Microsoft Store) is coming soon, along with iOS and Android apps. The web app has full feature parity and the best Coin prices.
Yes, VERGR is completely free to sign up and use. You can watch streams, post, message, join squads, and browse content without spending anything. Coins are optional — you only buy them if you want to tip creators, join a membership, unlock a paid stream, boost posts, or purchase marketplace services.

toll Coins & Gems

Coins are in-app currency — you buy them and spend them inside the app (tipping, memberships, paid streams, boosts, marketplace). Coins cannot be withdrawn by the buyer. Gems are the creator payout currency — you earn them when other people spend Coins on you (tips, memberships, paid stream access, service purchases). Gems are withdrawn as USDC to your own crypto wallet. 1,000 Gems = €10.00.
Apple and Google charge up to 30% fees on in-app purchases. We pass this cost to mobile prices to keep our margins sustainable. On web/desktop, we use Tebex with much lower fees — so you get the same Coins for ~40% less. We always recommend buying on web when possible.
You need a minimum of 1,000 Gems (€10.00) to withdraw. Go to your Wallet, tap "Withdraw," and enter your bank details. All withdrawals go through a 72-hour escrow hold for security. After the hold, the funds are sent to your bank via SEPA, ACH, or SWIFT. Bank processing fees are passed to you.
When Coins are spent on you, a commission is taken before they convert to Gems. New users start at 30% commission. As you level up (earning XP from activity), the rate drops — all the way down to 10% at Level 100. So a 100-Coin tip gives a new user 70 Gems, but a Level 100 user gets 90 Gems.

live_tv Live Streaming

Tap Go Live to broadcast straight from the browser, or stream in with OBS using your stream key. Viewers watch and chat inside VERGR, and can tip you during the stream. You can also link a Twitch, YouTube, or Kick stream instead.
Co-streaming puts a guest on screen with you, with gameplay kept in picture-in-picture. Both audiences watch the same show in one chat room. Co-streaming is available on the Pro plan.
A creator can set a stream to require payment to join, either as a one-off unlock in Coins or as part of a creator membership. Members keep access to that creator's paid streams for as long as the membership is active.

group Squads

A Squad is a group of up to 100 members with its own group chat, voice hangouts, and shared clips. A squad wallet holds coins contributed by members, and is distributed equally among the members on the 1st of each month.
Squad creation unlocks at Level 10. If you want to skip the level requirement, you can purchase a "Squad License" for €5.00. Once created, invite members through the app — your squad can have up to 100 members.
On the 1st of every month at midnight, a scheduled job reads your squad's wallet balance, divides it by 100, and credits each member's Gem balance equally. The squad wallet then resets to 0. This is fully automated — no action needed from squad leaders.

live_tv Streaming

VERGR doesn't host streams — instead, you link your existing Twitch, YouTube, or Kick channel. Go to your profile settings, tap "Linked Accounts," and connect your streaming platform. When you go live, your stream is embedded inside VERGR so your audience can watch without leaving the app.
VERGR connects directly to your Twitch chat via IRC/WebSocket. Your Twitch chat messages appear inside VERGR in real time, and VERGR users' messages are sent to your Twitch chat. This means cross-platform interaction — viewers on Twitch and viewers on VERGR are in the same conversation.

chat Messaging

Yes. Direct messages are temporarily stored on our servers only for delivery. Messages are saved locally on your device. Server-side message data is automatically deleted using TTL (Time To Live) policies. We do not read, scan, or analyse the content of private messages.
VERGR uses WebRTC for peer-to-peer voice and video calls. This means calls go directly between your devices using Google's STUN servers — we don't route your call through our servers. This keeps calls fast, private, and free.
Groups can have up to 1,000 members. Group messages work the same way as DMs — temporarily stored on the server for delivery, with local storage on each member's device.

storefront Marketplace

You can sell gaming-related services: coaching sessions, rank boosting, graphic design (overlays, logos), video editing, and more. Paid coaching unlocks at Level 30. All transactions use escrow — the buyer's Coins are held until the service is delivered and confirmed.
When a buyer places an order, their Coins are held in escrow. The seller completes the service and marks it as delivered. The buyer then confirms delivery, and the Coins convert to Gems (minus commission) in the seller's wallet. If there's a dispute, it goes to our moderation team.

settings Account & Safety

Your Conduct Score reflects how positively you interact with the community. Receiving "Helpful" and "Respect" reactions increases your score. A high Conduct Score (above 800) unlocks special rewards like the "The Helpful" title at Level 15, custom emotes at Level 25, and guardian priority at Level 60. Toxic behaviour lowers your score and can restrict access to these perks.
KYC (Know Your Customer) is identity verification required for withdrawals. Before you can cash out your Gems, you need to verify your identity — this is a legal requirement for financial transactions and helps us prevent fraud. You'll need to provide a government-issued ID and a selfie.
Tap on the user's profile, then tap the three-dot menu and select "Report." Choose the reason for reporting and provide any additional context. Our moderation team reviews all reports. Serious violations (cheating, fraud, harassment) can result in account suspension and Gem forfeiture.
